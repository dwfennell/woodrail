# Routes Phase 1: screenshots (phone, 390×844)

Branch routes-phase1 (head 5009516). A Sandbox map with a test line of depots.

2. `2-planning.png`: "Plan a route" mid-planning, after tapping the mill on the map ("Done" ends planning).

4. `4-dotted-path-unrouted.png`: a train with no route. The white dotted path along the top track is where its switches send it.
5. `5-cant-reach-red-x.png`: a stop no track leads to (Applebury) has a grey disc with a red ✕, and the card says "Can't reach Applebury: no track leads there".
6. `6-running.png`: the train running: "On its way to the mill".
