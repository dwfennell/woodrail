# Balance pass with everything in (2026-11-05)

A local merge of `claude/batch-fg` + `claude/mail` + `claude/civic` + `claude/metro` (which carries `claude/rack`),
used only for measuring. On it: the nine-seed `BALANCE=1` run (2027, 1–6, 9, 10) to minute 180, the same nine seeds
played on to minute 360, and payback sums from the game's own constants (`PRICES`, `RECIPES`, `THING_RULES`,
blueprints, `mailSeconds`), plus a sim run of the metro with a leisure place in its city. Each was compared against
batch-fg alone.

## The short of it

- **Bands: all nine seeds pass on the merge**, and its SUMMARY lines at 180 are byte-identical to batch-fg's on all
  nine (see "Merging mail" below for the one way that breaks). The balance player builds none of the post, places, metro
  or rack, so these numbers can't show their payoff. The payoff part below is worked out from the game's rules.
- **Coins pile up late, and the post makes it worse.** The harness player has 521–1297 coins at 180 and
  883–3585 at 360 (net 3–13 coins a minute after 180). A post office between towns brings in 32–80 coins a minute and
  pays for itself in 5–12 min. That is the best coin source in the game, and its inputs are free.
- **The metro and the places stack.** A metro rider getting off in a city with any leisure place counts as a
  visitor. A 1-coin fare × 1.5 rounds to 2, so **one cinema doubles the metro's income** (24.5 → 49 coins/min), and
  the line pays back in 54 min instead of the 114 its spec asks for ("pays back slowly").
- **Places cost more goods than their smiles save** until a town is three levels past City. Coins aren't short
  late but goods are, so smiles are a weak buy.
- **Research pacing**: the merge adds 23 blueprints (85 → 108). The late player fills its idle research with the new
  track rows (slips, double three-way, turntable controls, Mountain railway), which is harmless. The Reading room
  never comes into play for the harness player (no College by 360). The real late stall is older than this pass:
  after minute 180 the goods train's hoppers fill with coal that nothing takes, and Foundry waits 91–150 min for its
  last stone (seeds 4, 9, 10, the same on batch-fg alone).
- **Goods chains starving towns**: the harness doesn't build the chains that take planks (toy workshop, car factory,
  carpenter), so this can't be measured here. As the user said, workshop deliveries starving towns isn't a worry,
  and nothing in these runs points the other way.

### Places: goods in, goods saved

A smile cuts 5% off every good of each level a town grows (up to 4 smiles, 20%). Building a town's first four smiles
(town hall, park, fire and police stations) takes 52 goods (12 + 8 + 16 + 16) and 720 coins with their blueprints.
Four smiles save 16 goods at City ★, 26 at ★★ and 39 at ★★★. They pay back their goods partway to ★★★, and no town in
the 360-minute runs gets past ★. One smile (park, with the town hall it needs) is 20 goods for 4 a level.

## Tweaks

### Made (on `claude/balance-3`, off `claude/batch-h`)

`claude/batch-h` is batch-fg with civic, mail, metro and rack, perf-4 and track-pieces in, so everything below is on
it. (An earlier `claude/balance-2`, off batch-fg, had only the oil change.)

1. **Oil 9 → 7 coins** (`PRICES.oil`). With no input, an oil well made 38.6 coins a minute and paid back in 10 min,
   well above the other raw goods (milk, fruit, sugar, flowers, stone, water: 20–25). At 7 it makes 30 a minute and
   pays back in 13 min, near the dairy farm's.
2. **A ride within one town isn't a visitor's** (`visitorsFor` in `passengers.ts`: the visitor factor only when
   `from.townId !== to.townId`; the Day out count too). A metro ride across a city pays its plain fare with or
   without a cinema, so the line keeps its 114-min payback. Riders from other towns still pay ×1.5–2.5. Test:
   `places.test.ts`, "a ride within one town is no visitor's".
3. **Post office: one post every 10 s** (was 6 s). One office sorts two big cities' mail, at most 6 a minute (48
   coins). Two towns at Town level still post 4 a minute, all sorted, so mail's payback test (beside the orchard's and
   juice works') is unchanged. `post.test.ts`: one office sorts two big cities, not three.
4. **Places' goods halved** (rounded up), coins kept: town hall 6🪵, park 4🪵, fire and police 5🪵 3🪨, and so on
   (the civic spec's price line). A town's first four smiles now cost 26 goods and pay them back during City ★ → ★★.
   `e2e:civic` brings 6 and 4 planks (was 12 and 8); `places.test.ts` reads the town hall's planks from the table.
5. **Harness, `roomForMine`:** postal goods are skipped (a town gives mail). The town before the mine is no longer
   taken for the stop that gives goods.
6. **Harness, `clearUnwanted`** (the late coal jam): on the mine's card, Doesn't give for each of its goods no stop
   on the line takes now, and Gives again once one does. On a wagon's card, Empty (while the train stands) for a
   wagon that has carried goods no stop takes for 3 looks, and only while it could carry something a stop gives and
   another takes. `BALANCE_UNWANTED_MINUTES` sets the 3.
7. **Harness, `BALANCE_CIVIC=1`:** the player also learns Town hall, Park and Post office after Quarries. It builds a
   town hall and then a park in its first City through the card's Build (`startPlace`: coins paid, planks by train),
   and a post office by the line with a mail van on the goods train. Off by default, so the default run is unchanged.

### Proposed earlier (made above, as 2–7)

1. **civic + metro: riders who ride within one town aren't visitors.** In `fareFor`, apply `visitorsAt` only when
   `from.townId !== to.townId`. Then a metro ride across a city pays its plain fare, and the metro keeps its 114-min
   payback with or without a cinema. Visitors from other towns still pay ×1.5–2.5. (Without this, a stadium, ×2.5
   rounded to 3, makes the metro's riders pay 3 coins each, a payback of about 36 min.)
2. **mail: post office cycle 6 s → 10 s** (6 post a minute, at most 48 coins a minute an office). Two towns at Town
   level still sort all they post (4 a minute), so the payback in mail's own test band (about the orchard's and juice
   works') holds. Four big towns no longer make one office worth 80 a minute. The test "one post office sorts the post
   of three big cities" becomes "of two". Or, softer: post 8 → 6 coins (2 towns: 24 a minute, payback 16.5 min; mail's
   test band would need to widen a little).
3. **civic: halve places' goods** (town hall 12 → 6🪵, park 8 → 4🪵, fire and police 10🪵 6🪨 → 6🪵 3🪨, the rest
   alike), keeping the coins. Four smiles then cost 26 goods and pay them back during City ★ → ★★, inside a long game.
   The coins stay the sink the spec wants (about 720 a town with the blueprints).
4. **Harness (balance.test.ts), with mail:** `roomForMine` picks the nearest stop before the mine that gives goods.
   With mail, a town gives `mail`, so a town before the mine gets "Only drop" for mail (seeds 1, 2, 4, 5 at 360) and
   the quarry's stone is never fixed. Filter out postal goods there (`!POSTAL.has(k)`), as `harbour` and `school` do.
5. **Harness: the late coal jam.** After Mines and Foundry's research has its coal, hoppers stay full of coal no stop
   takes until a foundry stands, and the last stone for Foundry's research waits 91–150 min (seeds 4, 9, 10). It's the
   same on batch-fg alone. A player would turn off coal at the mine on its card (`givesChoice`) until a foundry or
   power station takes it; `unstick` should do so too. Until then, play past 180 is pessimistic on those seeds.
6. **A `BALANCE_CIVIC=1` / post mode** in the harness (the civic spec's §5 idea), building a post office, a town hall
   and a park through the card in the first City. It's the only way to show their payoff on the player's path (AGENTS.md).

## Merging mail (for whoever lands it)

- `PRICES.post` and `FULL_PAY`'s `"post"` must come through the conflict in `towns.ts`. Without them the balance
  player ranks Post office as a new goods chain (`rank` in `decide`), researches it on 4 of 9 seeds instead of
  Junctions, Engine shed or Tunnels, and the SUMMARY lines move. With them, every line matches batch-fg.
- Every array indexed by a good's index must be `CARGO_SLOTS` long (mail's goods are at 30–31): batch-fg's new
  `modes` for warehouses (`addDepot`), `keeps`, and the save schema's `piles` and `landed`.
- civic moves the save schema to `src/persist/save-schema.ts` and the CSG carving to `src/render/piece-carve.ts`.
  batch-fg's depot `tracks` (schema), the new depot roles, and the underpass plinth (`trenchBrush(def, plinth)`) go
  there, and so does metro's `rackStrip`.
- The icon font must be built again (`scripts/icon-font.py`) after the merge. It came to 26.4 kB for 107 icons, over
  the 24 kB budget in `icon-font.test.ts`.
