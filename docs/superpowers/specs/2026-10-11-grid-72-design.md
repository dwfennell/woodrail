# 72 mm grid: track that fits the map

**Status:** approved (user approved approach A and the design in chat, 2026-10-11)

## Problem

Track pieces follow wooden-rail sizes: straights of 54, 108, 144 and 216 mm (multiples of 18), large curves of
radius 182, small of 90. The map's squares are 64 mm, which no piece fits: a long straight covers 3.375 squares.
Towns are placed at arbitrary positions and any 45° heading, so a run between two stations rarely closes, and track
never lines up with hills, rivers or roads. Each quarter turn of large curves is also 2 mm off the 18 mm lattice.

## Goal

Pieces and the map share one rhythm, so connecting stations and running track along hills works out — without
turning layout into a plain grid exercise.

## Design

### Grid
- `CELL` 64 → 72 mm (4 × 18). `COLS × ROWS` 70 × 52 → 62 × 46 (4464 × 3312 mm, about the same mat).
- Blocks are 72 mm wide and stay `RISER_HEIGHT` (64 mm) tall: hill levels, ramps (216 mm, one riser) and risers keep
  their heights. A long straight, ramp, bridge or station covers exactly 3 squares; a 144 mm straight covers 2.

### Pieces
- `LARGE_CURVE_RADIUS` 182 → 180 (also the switch branch). A quarter turn of large curves (180 mm = 2½ squares each
  way) takes track centred along a row of squares to track centred along a column, with its end on a square edge.
- Straights, small curves (90 mm) and 45° geometry are unchanged. Small curves, the 108 and 54 mm straights and
  diagonals land between grid lines — that is the room for craft.

### Stations
- Station headings are multiples of 90° (no diagonal stations).
- A town's centre (the station's midpoint) is a square's centre: the station track runs down the middle of a row or
  column, and its two ends lie on square edges.
- The starter pair (centres x = ±540) already lies on this lattice; their z snaps to a square centre.

### Surroundings
- Scenery blocks use the same proportions: near band 72 × 64 mm, far band twice that (144 × 128). `OuterBlock`
  gains `height`; stacks are `level × height`.

### Unchanged
- Joint play stays 9 mm and 4°. Overlap clearance stays.
- `SAVE_VERSION` 3 → 4 (old saves no longer load; agreed acceptable before v1).

## Testing
- On 200 maps, every station's heading is a multiple of 90°, its centre is a square centre and its ends are on
  square edges.
- Straights between the ends of two same-facing stations on one row close exactly (no joint error).
- Two large curves turning 90° end with their peg on a square edge, centred on a column.
- Blocks are 72 wide and 64 tall; scenery stacks use their own block height.
