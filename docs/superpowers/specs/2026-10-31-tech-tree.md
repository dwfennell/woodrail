today, where it runs thin, and proposes 18 new nodes in small steps, starting with ones that only gate things that
already exist.

`tray-catalogue.ts`, `src/track/kits.ts`, `pieces.ts`, the treats plan (`plans/2026-10-24-town-treats-plan.md`,

`BALANCE=1 BALANCE_SEED=2027`.

### Gaps
1. **Two key nodes can never be learned.** Town hall and Park are shown from minute 0 but are "coming soon". A child
   sees two of the first dozen tags locked forever.
2. **Two whole branches are empty:** Town life and Leisure have no built node. Water has two, both tier 3.
3. **Dead ends early.** Signals, halts, scenery, bridges, turntable, farming2, mills2 and dock-sheds lead nowhere built.
   Signals' only child (telegraph) is planned; bridges' (tunnels) too.
4. **Three upgrade ladders are the same idea** (harvesters, sawmills, quarries: "×1.5, then ×2"). Useful, not exciting.
5. **16 Sandbox kits aren't in Game mode at all:** two-platform station, grand terminus, fuel station, log loader,
   grain elevator, cargo conveyor, coal chute, gantry crane, harbour crane, magnet crane, tipping dump, drop hopper,
   goods shed, loop-the-loop, train lift, buffer-stop lamp. The models, the track and the e2e (`e2e:kits`) exist.
6. **Research is plank-bound, coins pile up.** Every cost has planks; stalls are "short of planks"; coins reach 1279 by
   180 with nothing to buy. Late game (after foundry, smithy, electric, harbours) has nothing to aim for: the rocket
   needs three planned nodes (observatory, trade, university).

## 3. Pacing

Bands (game-mode.md): City 60–90 min; research stall ≤ 15 min; researched-by-180 floors. Baseline City minute:
2027 62 (64 in today's run), 1 61, 2 73, 3 80, 4 80, 5 73, 6 67, 9 69, 10 82.

**Rule: nothing new may take planks before the first City.** Planks are the bottleneck before 60–80 min. So:

| Lands | Nodes | Why there |
|---|---|---|
| 0–60 (before City) | Q1 study hall, T2 fun track, I1 loading kits, R4 brake vans | t1, 4–5🪵, looks or speed only. Only if the balance player doesn't pick them (it follows its own list), they move no SUMMARY lines; players who pick them spend a few planks for fun. |
| 60–120 (first City, coins arrive) | T1, T3, T5, R1, R3, C1, C2, C3, S1 | Coin-heavy t2: soaks the coins that reach 1279 by 180 without competing for planks. |
| 120–180 | R2, I3, I4, Q2, S2, T4 | Bigger features; R2 and Q2 shift balance on purpose. |
| 180–440 (long part) | I2 big cranes; then today's t3 | Gives the long part targets while waiting on City ★. |

Keeping research flowing:
- Seed 2027 has ten 5+ minute "nothing to do" stretches with research waiting on planks. Coin-priced nodes give a ready
  choice in those stretches without lengthening the plank stall.
- R2 and Q2 change cargo flow and school intake: each needs the nine-seed bands before and after, a table in the commit,
  and a re-check of the stall band. Never loosen a band.
- **Card luck:** a new piece type shifts every later crate draw (curved-ramps did). Re-gated kits are already piece
  types, but adding them to `THING_RULES` puts them in `crateFor`'s pool once learned. Try `BALANCE_CARD_SEED`.
- **Goals play-through:** none of these are goals, so the 22 goals and allowlists don't change. If C1 or C3 gets a goal
  (the treats plan has one), it must be reached by the player's path (`treatRow` → buy), not by writing state.

## Decisions (the user, 2026-10-31)
- **Next goods chain: dairy → bakery, as a richer shape.** A dairy farm makes milk. A bakery takes **flour + milk** and makes bread for towns. That reuses the farm → mill chain, and gives a two-into-one step at the end of a three-step chain. Later chains should use the same richer shapes (X → Y → Z, or (A + B) → C), e.g. orchard → (fruit + sugar) → jam.
- Also accepted:
  1. bring the 16 Sandbox-only kits into Game mode behind research;
  2. price new nodes mostly in coins;
  3. move the caboose to its own Trains node;
  4. treats and the paint shop open by town level, as plan a57a272 has it;
  5. bogie wagons later, at a release point with the nine seeds;
  6. hide Town hall and Park until they're built.
