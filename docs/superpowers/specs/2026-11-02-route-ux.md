# Route creation and editing: the UX roadmap (2026-11-02)

**Where it comes from:** the audit in [docs/design/2026-11-02-route-ux](../../design/2026-11-02-route-ux/README.md)
(19 numbered fixes, F1–F19). This is the plan for the player's side of routes (cards, the map, goals); how routed
trains run is the routing spec's (`2026-10-30-train-routing.md`).

**The bar** (the user, 2026-11-02): "apply all recommendations and tidy things up until this is a great experience."
- Impeccable critique ≥ 32/40 and audit ≥ 17/20, re-run on the audit's map (`src/play/bugmaps/route-stuck.txt`).
- "Make a 2-stop route" found with no hints in about a minute (estimated as the audit did).
- Phone first: 44 px targets, nothing clipped at 360 px, nothing per frame. Route-keep, `firstStopAhead` and via
  semantics stay as they are.

**Coordination:** `claude/route-follow-lines` (shortcut highlight, first-come slots: R9 ties in) and
`claude/warehouse-controls` (the route card's notDrops/notPicks lines). Merge origin/main before each report.

## Steps

Each step lands alone, with an e2e on the player's path that checks its payoff (AGENTS.md). Audit numbers in brackets;
some are merged where they touch the same thing.

### Phase A: getting out of mistakes, and the quick wins (done on `claude/route-ux-fixes`)

| Step | Fix | Done when |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | Stop modes as three choices, Pass by its own button, the mode on the chip [F2] | Tapping the mode any number of times never takes a stop off; Only drop delivers and loads nothing there (e2e `route-ux`) |
| A2 | Leave asks before it forgets a line, any name [F3] | The first tap asks, the second forgets (e2e) |
| A3 | ▶ means one thing: chips keep their numbers, the next filled; ← → move [F7] | No ▶ on the card; numbers 1…n; one `aria-current` (e2e) |
| A4 | Frame the whole route while planning and after Done [F5] | Zoomed in on the last place tapped, the whole route is in view above the bar; after Done, above the card (e2e) |
| A5 | Vias findable: hint, "+ via", via chips shown on the map, a tap beside a station is the station [F6] | A via picked off screen is brought into view; a tap 20 mm past a station adds it (e2e) |
| A6 | Undo for route edits, and while planning [F1] | Undo after a stop added puts the route back, the train heading where it did; Leave and Pass by undone (unit + e2e, touch and Ctrl+Z) |
| A7 | The learn toast says "Tap a train, then Plan a route" [F4, toast] | The toast and the "Next:" toast say it |

### Phase B: finding routes, and reading the map

| Step | Fix | Done when |
|---|---|---|
| B1 | A goal after Train routes: "Give a train a route", with Show me pointing at a train [F4, goal] | Reached in `balance.test.ts` by taps' own paths (engine, Plan a route, places); goals play-through passes; `e2e:research` and `e2e:tutorial` pass; where it sits in the goal order said |
| B2 | Words and contrast: "Wait to fill", "line" in status texts where the train's on one, ✎ on the line's name, place numbers lighter; Done in go-green with dark ink, a lost stop at 0.7 and struck through, route colours as DESIGN.md tokens [F17, F18] | Every route text ≥ 4.5:1; no hard-coded route colours outside tokens |
| B3 | Line colours that show on wood: a dark edge under each ribbon (≥ 3:1 against beech) [F10]; a lacquer cap on line chips, not the 5 px stripe [F11] | Contrast of each ribbon's edge against `#e7c89a` ≥ 3:1 (unit); the detector's "side tab" gone |

| B9 | Landscape: the picked stop's tools scrolled into view [F16]; desktop keys: Enter for Done, Delete for the picked stop, ← → to move it [F19] | No scroll to reach a stop's tools at 844×390; keys do as said (e2e:click) |
