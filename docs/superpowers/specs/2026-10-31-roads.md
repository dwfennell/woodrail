# Roads in Game mode (2026-10-31)

A proposal; steps 1–3 are built (branch `claude/roads-1`, see "Decisions" and §7). The user answered §6 on
2026-11-01: every question as suggested. The user: "we are also missing anything to do with roads, could be interesting at some point
soon". This surveys what roads can do today, then proposes a small Roads branch for the research tree: fun for kids,
priced mostly in coins (tech-tree decision 2), and cheap on phones.

Sources: `src/roads/*` (pieces, place, auto, grow-town, lanes), `src/game/road-hand.ts`, `street-moves.ts`,
`render/dressing/roads.ts`, `render/crossings-view.ts`, `landscape/crossings.ts`, `landscape/crossing-styles.ts`,
`ui/tray-catalogue.ts`, `scripts/e2e/roads.mjs`, the road system spec (`specs/2026-10-12-road-system-design.md`), Life on
the Line (`specs/2026-10-11-life-on-the-line-design.md`), game-mode.md (#18) and the tech-tree spec with its decisions.

## 1. Roads today

| Thing | Sandbox | Game mode |
|---|---|---|
| **Road pieces** (Roads tab): auto road, straight, bend, T, crossroads, dead end, zebra, road bridge (river only), ramp halves | yes, free | **no** (`modes: SANDBOX`; e2e:roads K1 checks it) |
| **Country lanes**: generated from most towns' platform side to the mat edge (`SURF_LANE`, pieces fitted by `fitLaneRoads`) | yes | yes |
| **Town streets** (`SURF_ROAD`): laid by `growTown` as a town grows | static | grow with the town; a street can be dragged within its town (#18, `street-moves.ts`) |
| **Level crossings**: wherever ground-level track crosses a lane or a player's road (`findCrossings`). Arms drop while a car is within 260 mm | yes | yes |
| **Crossing styles**: a "level crossing" prop (striped booms, flashing lamps) or "gated crossing" (white field gates) dropped on a crossing | free | free once **Crossing gates** (D3) is learned (Scenery kit before step 2) |
| **Road vehicles** | none | none |

What the pieces already give us for free:
- **A road graph.** Pieces are data (ports and links, `pieces.ts`), saved as one byte per square. The road spec (§5)
  planned vehicles on exactly this: a node per square, an edge per pair of matched ends. Nothing builds it yet.
- **Placement rules and messages** (`RoadPlacer`, `ROAD_MESSAGES`): rivers need a bridge, track only crosses a plain
  straight, a station's way in takes only a straight across the line, nothing on town streets.
- **Crossing state.** `CrossingsView` already knows each frame a train runs whether a crossing is shut. Vehicles can
  read the same answer instead of working it out again.
- **e2e:roads** covers laying, turning, moving, undo, bridges, ramps, crossings, saves and an idle scene (0–1 frames
  in 5 s).

What's missing in Game mode: the player can't lay a road, roads do nothing for the economy, and nothing drives on
them. Lanes go to the mat edge, not to other towns.

## 2. Proposed: a Roads branch (6 nodes)

A new branch, `roads`, between Passengers and Town life. Size: **S** a row and gates; **M** a small feature; **L** new
sim or goods. Roads are owned by Rail and road; Game Mode adds the rows, prices and payoffs.

| # | Node | Unlocks · kid-clear line | Cost · needs · requires | Size | Perf risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | **Roads** | the Roads tab in Game mode: auto road, straight, bend, T, crossroads, dead end. Each square costs 2🪙 · "Lay roads between your towns." | t2, 2🪵+60🪙 · passengers · a village | S | None new: the Sandbox code; a count of road squares cached by the grid's revision. |
| D2 | **Linked towns** (comes with D1, no extra row) | a town joined by road to another town wants 10% less to grow; its card says "Joined by road to Fernby" · "Towns joined by a road grow faster." | — | M | A flood fill over road squares, run once per road edit (keyed by a roads version), never per frame. |
| D3 | **Crossing gates** | the two crossing styles move here from Scenery, plus a zebra crossing · "Gates that close the road when a train comes." | t2, 2🪵+40🪙 · D1 | S (re-gate) | None: the styles exist. |
| D4 | **Road bridges** | road bridge over a river and the ramp halves · "Roads over rivers and up hills." | t2, 3🪵+80🪙 · D1, bridges | S (re-gate) | None. |
| D5 | **Delivery vans** | a van at a station's yard carries its town's goods the last stretch, by road, to a town or industry up to 8 squares away that the track doesn't reach · "Vans take goods from the station to towns off the line." | t2, 4🪵+150🪙 · D1, warehouses | L | The real one; see §3. |
| D6 | **Buses** | a bus stop prop beside a station; a bus brings people from a town joined by road to the station, for passenger trains · "A bus brings people to your trains." | t2, 120🪙 · D5, halts | M (on D5's machinery) | As D5. |

Later, not proposed now: a **road over the line** (an overbridge: a road on a span over track, for crossings a kid
wants without gates). It needs a new road piece, clearance under it and art; L, with Rail and road. Today track
already goes **over** a road on a bridge or riser (`OVER`), so "a bridge where road and rail meet" works one way round.

Why each is fun:
- **D1/D2** give coins a use after the first City (coins reach ~1279 by 180) and make a map look joined up, the way a
  Brio set does with its road pieces. Nothing in today's economy is asked of the player: it's a bonus.
- **D3** gates already close for trains; moving them here gives the branch a cheap, visible step.
- **D5** is the payoff: rail stays the long haul, vans the last stretch. A town behind a hill or a river, or a farm
  far from the line, gets served without a track spur. Vans never carry between two stations (no competing with rail).
- **D6** gives passengers a reason to build a station near, but not in, a town.

## 3. Road vehicles, cheap

Rules for D5 and D6 (and the AGENTS.md performance rules):
- **No physics.** Vans and buses are not Rapier bodies. Each is a distance along a cached polyline (`t += v·dt`), posed
  from it. No collisions between vehicles: at most one van per station yard and one bus per stop, each on its own run.
- **Paths are worked out on a road edit, not per trip.** A breadth-first search over the road graph from each yard or
  stop to the towns and industries it serves, cached by the roads version and the set of stations. Up to 8 squares, so
  the search is tiny. A road taken away invalidates the cache; a van mid-trip with no road ahead goes back.
- **Instanced.** One `InstancedMesh` per model (van, bus), one draw call each however many there are. A cap of 8 vans
  and 4 buses per map.
- **Nothing when idle.** A vehicle moves only while it has a load to take (or is coming back). A parked van is a still
  instance: no frames, no work. While any vehicle moves, the game counts it as an animation, as a train is.
- **Shadows.** A moving van would redraw shadows (`moveShadows()`) every frame it moves. Give vehicles a baked blob
  shadow under the instance and keep them out of the shadow casters, so they never cost a shadow pass.
- **Crossings.** A van comes to a crossing's square, asks the crossing (shared with `CrossingsView`) if it's shut, and
  waits at the square's edge if so. A van on a crossing never stops a train: trains don't look at roads.
- **Logic off the render loop.** Loads, timers and arrivals tick in the economy's update (like unloading), so the
  balance harness can run them headless with no render.
- **Measure:** idle frames (still 0–1 in 5 s with parked vans), frame time with 8 vans moving on a 360×740 phone, and
  the path rebuild on a road edit (target under 1 ms).

## 4. Balance and tests

- **D1, D3, D4** don't change the balance player (it lays no roads): SUMMARY lines should be byte-identical.
- **D2** changes growth only for players who lay roads. If the harness learns to lay a road between two towns (by the
  player's path: Roads learned, pieces bought with coins through `RoadPlacer`), run the nine seeds and report the City
  minute shift. Never loosen a band.
- **D5/D6** move goods and people: nine seeds, both goals parts, a table in the commit.
- **Stickers, not goals** (decision 5): "Join two towns by road" is a sticker (`stickers.ts`), not a core goal, so the
  goals play-through and its allowlist don't change. A test earns it by laying roads through the player's path
  (`RoadHand`, coins paid), not by writing road bytes.
- **e2e on the player's path** (extend `e2e:roads`, or a new `e2e:road-game`): learn Roads, lay a road between two
  towns, check coins went down by the price, the town card says "Joined by road", and the town's want drops. For D5:
  a train unloads at a station, the van drives out, the town off the line **receives the goods** and grows.
- K1 in `e2e:roads` changes: Game mode has no Roads tab **until Roads is learned**.

## 5. Build order (each lands alone)

1. **D1 Roads (S), built:** a `roads` branch and row; the Roads tab in Game mode once learned; road squares bought
   with coins (`road-box.ts`, not `THING_RULES`: a road square is a byte on the grid, not a thing); taken up, undone or
   cleared, a square goes back in the box (see Decisions). Tests: `roads-game.test.ts`, e2e:roads K1–K8.
2. **D3, D4 re-gates (S), built:** crossing styles and zebra to D3 (out of Scenery), bridge and ramps to D4. SUMMARY
   identical (nine seeds).
3. **D2 Linked towns (M), built:** road graph + town flood fill (`roads/graph.ts`, cached by the grid's revision), the
   growth bonus credited at delivery (§7), the card line, the "Join two towns by road" sticker. Tests:
   `linked-towns.test.ts`; e2e:roads M1–M5 (lay between two towns from the tray, joined, card, sticker, bonus).
4. **A van that only drives (M):** the vehicle layer (instanced, path cache, blob shadow, crossing waits), driving a
   van between two joined towns for show. Measure idle and moving frame time before going on.
5. **D5 Delivery vans (L):** the van takes goods from a station's yard to an off-line town or industry. Bands.
6. **D6 Buses (M):** the bus stop prop and the bus, carrying passengers to a station.
7. **Overbridge (L):** later, with Rail and road, if wanted.

Steps 1–3 are about a day and give a learnable branch with a payoff. Steps 4–6 are the bigger, riskier part.

## Decisions (the user, 2026-11-01)

All six §6 questions answered as suggested:

1. **Roads cost 2🪙 a square** (`ROAD_COINS`). Built as a box, the way track works: the squares you own are
   `Progress.roads` (saved; the map's own lanes count as owned from the start), and what's free to lay is owned less
   what's on the map. A square beyond the box is bought as it goes down. Taken up (put back in the tray, removed from
   its card, undone) it goes back in the box, free to lay again: coins aren't given back, as a track piece taken up
   goes back in the toy box, not back to planks. An undo or redo that would need a square you can't pay for is
   refused ("Road: needs 1🪙 more"), as a drop is. The tray's road items show the box's count, or 2🪙 when it's empty.
2. **Linked towns grow a bit faster** (the 10% want cut, D2).
3. **The crossing styles move from Scenery to the Roads branch** (D3, Crossing gates). Scenery kit keeps its sights and
   furniture.
4. **Vans and buses are wanted, as feeders from a station only**, never between two towns on their own (D5, D6: rows on
   the board, coming soon).
5. **"Join two towns by road" is a sticker**, not a core goal.
6. **Lanes to the mat edge stay as they are.** No lanes laid between towns by the map.

What changed on main since the proposal, and how the rows fit it: the board's tier-2 rows take coins when they start
(as here); stickers exist now (`stickers.ts`, ten so far), so decision 5's sticker goes there; Scenery kit
(`scenery`, Crafting, t1 ← Carpenter's bench) no longer lists crossing gates; Bridges is "Bridges and ramps"
(`bridges`, Track, t1). The new rows: `roads` (D1), `road-crossings` (D3), `road-bridges` (D4), `vans` (D5, soon),
`buses` (D6, soon, ← vans, halts), in a new Roads branch after Passengers. D2 has no row (it comes with D1). D3 takes
2🪵 as well as its 40🪙: every built blueprint is researched with goods brought to a school (`research.test.ts`).

## 7. Built so far, and next

**Steps 1 and 2** (`claude/roads-1`):
- `blueprints.ts`: the Roads branch and its five rows. D1 and D4 bring no `THING_RULES` things (road pieces are grid
  bytes), so each names a `feature` for the board.
- `road-box.ts`: `ROAD_COINS`, `ROAD_TECH` (the blueprint each road piece needs), `roadsOnMap` (a scan of the grid
  cached by its revision: worked out again only after a square changes, never per frame), `roadsAdded` (how many
  squares an edit adds, from its changes alone), `roadsPrice`.
- `RoadHand.till` (Game mode): an edit adding more squares than the box holds is refused when coins are short, and so
  is its redo (or the undo of a removal). `settleRoads` in main pays for what went down, after the edit.
- The Roads tab: `TrayTab.gameTech` and `Hud.tabKnown` show it in Game mode once Roads is learned (`refreshTabs`, on
  learning or loading, cheap if no tab came or went). The zebra, bridge and ramp items are `unlearned: "hide"` by
  `ROAD_TECH`.
- Balance: the harness lays no roads and doesn't pick the new rows (`others` are taken in board order, and these come
  after everything it picks), so SUMMARY lines are byte-identical on the nine seeds.

**Step 3, D2 (built after the catch below was approved):**
- `roads/graph.ts`: `findTownLinks` floods from each town's street squares over road squares whose pieces meet
  (`roadsJoin`, heights and ramps included; an old street square with no piece counts as a crossroads) and lists the
  other towns each reaches. `townLinks` caches it by the grid's revision and town count: 0.05–0.07 ms a map, worked
  out only when asked after a change. No generated map (the starter and 15 seeds) comes with two towns joined.
- `Economy.roadGot` (saved as `roadGot`, absent when empty): what a town received while `joinedByRoad` (set by
  `TownLife.reset`, so the play-throughs get the same rule). `roadBonus` = ⌊roadGot ÷ 9⌋ of each good
  (`ROAD_BONUS_EVERY`); `growthOf` = received + bonus, and every level and want reads it (`townStatus(growthOf(…))`:
  goals, passengers, stickers, the ledger, landmarks, rewards, TownLife). What a town is told it received stays what
  arrived. Credited only as goods arrive and never taken back, so a road taken up never lowers a level.
- The town card: a "Road" row, "Joined by road to Fernby: grows faster" (`roadLine`; `ledgerKey` mixes in the grid's
  revision and `roadGot`).
- The sticker "On the road" (`road-link`, building): checked on the new "land" dep (the grid's revision).
- Balance: no generated map has joined towns and the harness lays no roads, so SUMMARY lines stay byte-identical (nine
  seeds).

**The catch (found while building steps 1 and 2).** A town's level is worked out from everything it has received (`townStatus(received)`, 14
callers), not kept. A want cut that depends on a road *now* would make a town's level go down when its road is taken
up (houses already built, `bestLevel`, research gates and stickers all read the level). So the 10% must be locked in
as it's earned: at delivery, a town joined by road is credited a bonus toward growth (one unit for every nine it
receives, kept beside `deliveredGoods` and saved), and `townStatus` adds the bonus to what was received. Nothing is
taken back if the road goes later. The road graph and town flood fill (`roads/graph.ts`) stay as planned, cached by
the grid's revision, asked at delivery time and by the town card, never per frame.
