# 72 mm Grid Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Map squares, blocks, large curves and stations share the 18 mm rhythm of the track pieces.

**Architecture:** Change `CELL` to 72 and the mat to 62 × 46; blocks keep `RISER_HEIGHT` as their height, so block
meshes take a separate height. Large curves become radius 180. Town placement snaps station midpoints to square
centres with 90° headings. Scenery blocks gain an explicit height.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, Three.js, Vitest.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-11-grid-72-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- `CELL` = 72, `COLS` = 62, `ROWS` = 46; `RISER_HEIGHT` stays 64.
- `LARGE_CURVE_RADIUS` = 180; straights, small curve (90) and 45° sweep unchanged.
- Station headings multiples of 90°; station midpoint (town centre) on a square centre.
- Joint play stays 9 mm / 4°.
- `SAVE_VERSION` = 4.

## Review Focus

- A station near the mat edge after snapping: it must still sit fully on the mat with its corridor (snapping moves
  it ≤ 36 mm; `EDGE_MARGIN` is 4 squares) — covered by the existing reachability retry; Task 3's test checks bounds.
- Town spacing after snapping: two towns may end up < `MIN_TOWN_SPACING` apart by up to ~51 mm — check spacing on
  the snapped centre (Task 3 implementation does).
- Hills under track: `groundUnder` samples per cell; with wider cells a piece on a hill edge reads the right height —
  existing clearance tests cover this once they pass at 72.
- Scenery tops: trees on scenery blocks stand at `h × height`, not `h × size` (Task 4 test).
- Old saves: version 3 saves are refused cleanly, starting the starter map (existing save tests, Task 5).
