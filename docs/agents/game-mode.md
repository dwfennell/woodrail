# Game Mode — playbook

## 1. Scope
The play layer on top of the railway:
- **Economy** (`src/economy/economy.ts`): depots, `RECIPES`, piles, caps, unloading (`canUnload`, `takesNow`), routes and stops (`picksAt`, `roomAhead`, `usedAfter`), and `owed` goods.
- **Workshop and crafting** (`src/game/crafting.ts`): `shopNeeds`, `shopOwes`, `shopOwing`, `builtOf`, `shopSpare`, `yardKeeps`, `keepsForOrder`, orders.
- **Goals** (`src/game/goals.ts`), **research** (`src/game/research.ts`, school, `SCHOOL_SHARE`) and **rewards** (`src/game/rewards.ts`).
- **Industries and map sites:** `industries.ts` (`SITE_RULES`, fields), `map-sites.ts` (ruins, `farmFields`, `placeSite`), `restore.ts` (`restoreRuin`, `restorePrice`, `trackCanReach`, payoff text), `harbours.ts` (`HarbourTrade`).
- **Balance:** `src/play/balance.test.ts`, the harness, the goals play-through and the bands.
- **Town looks as they grow** (issue #17): `src/props/storeys.ts` (storeys and styles by level, which houses rise), `townProps` / `setTownStoreys` / `sharesOut` in `src/props/towns.ts`, the stacked models in `src/render/props/library.ts`.

Rail and road owns the sim and sections. Send them head-ons and switch-path issues with a minimal repro; don't fix their code.

## 2. How to test
**Checks:** typecheck, the relevant vitest files, then one full `npm test` before handing off. Under load, unrelated tests time out: rerun them alone and say so. Never call a red run green, and don't pkill by name.

**Balance harness:** `BALANCE=1 npx vitest run src/play/balance.test.ts`. It isn't part of `npm test`.
- **Seeds:** `BALANCE_SEED=n`. The standard nine are 2027, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 9 and 10; in parallel they take about 4 min.
- **What it plays:** 180 min, one train per line. It prints SUMMARY and BANDS lines.
- **Bands:**
  - City between 60 and 90 min;
  - town gap under 45 min;
  - research stall 15 min or less (with a plank-famine exemption);
  - researched count at or above each seed's floor;
  - coins at 180 of 150–600 (nice-to-have).
  - **Never loosen a band.** A failing band is a finding.
- **Compare:** a pure harness or UI change must leave the SUMMARY lines byte-identical. For game changes, report the shifts in a table.
- **Baseline, City minute (default settings):** 2027 62, 1 61, 2 73, 3 80, 4 80, 5 73, 6 67, 9 69, 10 82. **Researched by 180:** 15, 17, 15, 15, 14, 15, 14, 15, 15.
- **Flags:**
  - `BALANCE_SPUR=1`: off by default; all 9 seeds pass with it on;
  - `BALANCE_SPARE_COVERS=off`;
  - `BALANCE_CARD=first|last` and `BALANCE_CARD_SEED=n`: card-luck robustness;
  - debug: `BALANCE_GOALS_LOG`, `BALANCE_JOIN_TRACE`, `BALANCE_STOPS`, `BALANCE_NOUNSTICK`, `BALANCE_JAM_MINUTES`, `BALANCE_SECOND_GOODS`, `BALANCE_LOOP_PIN`.

**Goals play-through:**
- **Fast part:** in `npm test`, to minute 175, about 50 s (keep it under 60 s). 20 of 23 goals ("Buy some land" at minute 23).
- **Long part:** `GOALS_LONG=1`, to minute 440, about 135–160 s. 23 of 23 (the player buys the plot Show me frames once "Buy some land" is the goal: `buyLand`, goals play-through only). No known breaks (`GOALS_LONG_KNOWN_BREAKS` is empty again: the `wrongRoad` from minute 223 on the map with that plot was a routing bug, fixed on `claude/fix-wrongroad-plot`, see rail-road.md). The mine's goods first ride at 368 of 440 (main before the land goal: 364; 435 with the routing bug, whose wrong road every lap held Harbours back from 255 to 269 and the harbour from 323 to 372): `roomForMine` walks back to the nearest stop with goods before the mine (goals play-through only), as a town sits just before it on this line. Thin: see open item 3. The CoS's gate runs it for economy, goals and industry changes.
- **Allowlists:** `GOALS_NOT_REACHED` and `GOALS_LONG_NOT_REACHED` (now empty), each entry with a reason. A guard test fails on unmet goals that aren't listed, stale entries, entries with no reason, or a long list that isn't a subset of the fast one.
- **The player's route:**
  - ruins are restored with `restoreRuin`, the price paid;
  - goods arrive by train;
  - never write piles, coins or learned directly.

**Footprints:** use `loadFootprints(["bldg"])` from `src/props/footprints-testing.ts`. Never `footprintOf: () => undefined`.

**e2e:** `npm run e2e:sites` covers ruins and restores. It takes about 37 min, and its late sections have crashed under load.

- **Research queue (#14).** `Research` keeps `current`, `next` and `later[]` (the queue is `next` then `later`, at most `QUEUE_MAX` = 5 waiting; `later` is empty while `next` is null). Player taps go through `toggleQueue` (start if nothing's under way or waiting; else add to the end; tap again removes) and `startNow` ("Research now": swaps it in, the one under way waits up next keeping its goods). The balance player still uses `pick`, unchanged (it never queues past `next`), so SUMMARY lines don't move. **Coins are paid when a queued item starts**, not when queued: nothing is reserved, so a queued item short of coins waits at the front (in order, "Waiting for N🪙") and the ones behind wait too. **Schools take goods for `current` only** (the issue's "how deep" setting is not built: it shifts balance). Chains: see the next item. Saved as `later` in `ResearchData` (absent when empty; old saves load with no queue). Cards: ready → "Research" / "Add to queue" ("The queue is full (5)"); queued → "✕ Remove" and "Research now", line "Next up, after X" / "2nd in line, after X"; web tag "Next up" / "In line: N"; pill "+N" badge (`data-more`, CSS only); school card "· N in queue".
- **Chains in the research queue (2026-11-02, `claude/research-chain-queue`).** The queue is always in order: each blueprint waits behind the ones it `needs` that aren't learned (or with them under way). In `research.ts`: `missingNeeds` (unlearned prerequisites, through theirs, prerequisites first), `chainOf` (what a tap adds: those not under way or queued, then it), `chainBlock`, `nowTarget`, `dequeue`, `repairQueue`.
  - **Queueing:** a tap on a locked card that needs blueprints queues the chain in one go: "Queue Farming, then Mills" ("Queue Switches and 2 more" for longer, its line saying the order; "Add to queue" / "After Farming" once Farming is under way or queued). With nothing under way or waiting it's "Research Farming now" ("Then Mills"): the first starts. Held back (`blocked`, with why) where the queue hasn't room ("Needs 2 places in the queue (1 left)") or a blueprint in the chain can't be researched.
  - **Requirements besides blueprints** (`requires`: a town, a city, a count) block queueing as well as starting, for the card itself and every blueprint in its chain: the queue can't bring them about, and `promote` drops what can't start. A card held by its own requirement has no research card (its "Needs a town" line, as before); one whose chain is held by another's says so ("Carpenter's bench needs 50 planks delivered first").
  - **Removing** takes out every queued one that needs it, directly or through another (`dequeue`), and main toasts "Removed Mills too" (`removedText`, short names).
  - **Research now:** never on a card whose prerequisites aren't learned. A queued one shows "Research Farming now" (`nowTarget`: the first of them that can start), which swaps Farming in like Research now and moves the rest of the chain and it up behind, in order; only "✕ Remove" while what it needs is under way. There is no other reordering.
  - **Saves:** unchanged format. On load (`Progress.fromJSON`) `repairQueue` moves one out of order back behind what it needs and drops one whose prerequisite isn't queued (or not in the game). It also runs where the balance player's `pick` replaces the one up next, and when `promote` drops one. `seenOf` shows every queued blueprint.

**Towns grow upward (#17), the middle only:**
- Only some houses rise (the user: "not _all_ buildings should get more levels… some should stay single story, more towards the edges"). A house's place in its town is its share of the town's buildings nearer the centre than it (`sharesOut`: 0 the nearest, 1 the farthest), so it works whatever the town's shape. It rises if its own draw (`chanceOf(id)`, fixed per building) is under the town's chance there (`tallChance`): 1 inside the band's start, 0 past its end, a smoothstep between (`tallBand`: village 0.05–0.45, town 0.15–0.6, city 0.25–0.75, ★ and on 0.4–0.8). About a quarter of a village's houses, half a city's; the outer fifth never. One that rises gains its level less one per ring (`RING`, 1.5 squares) out past `INNER` (2.5 squares), at least one; capped per model (cottages and bungalows +1, villas, terraces and semis +2, the apartment block +2). Never fewer at a higher level (the band only widens), and `setTownStoreys` never lowers a house at a level above 0.
- A building counts only the levels since it went up: `Building.born` (saved in the landscape, set by `buildOnLots` in play) is its town's level then, so each level's new buildings start single-storey.
- Growth puts up 2–4 buildings a level (`housesBy`) where the road plan (`growTown`, shared with map generation) says: along the outer streets, a new street behind the station, a gap near it; then greens and the town's border (`spreadLots`). All through `LandEditor.checkLot` (grass or green, no feature, one height, no track, no prop, not a station's way in, nothing cut off) and only on the map (the player's land). Not changed by the "middle only" work (a change there moves new maps' towns and the balance harness).
- Save v15: a v14 save's town houses load with no storeys (`TOWN_STOREYS_FROM`), and `TownLife.reset` gives them again by this rule (so an old all-tall town takes the new shape once; a house moved by #18 gets the rule's storeys where it stands).
