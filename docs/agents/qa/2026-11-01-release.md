Researched by minute 180, per seed (2027, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 9, 10):
- before: 15 13 13 13 13 11 13 12 11;
- main now: 16 15 13 11 12 14 14 12 14;
- Shares 0.6: 13 12 13 11 13 13 12 14 12.

Research pace is up with Research first. With the default Shares with towns it's about level, but stalls get longer.

**Cause of the big town gaps (seed 5, main now; `BALANCE_CARGO=76-84`).** Thimble Cross becomes a city at 73.6 min and sits at 0/22 planks until after 145 min. The other towns are full, the forest has 12 logs and the sawmill has planks. The train passes the city with 4 planks that the research (Signals, 4) lacks, kept for the school under Research first. Those planks stay in its two timber wagons round the line, through the forest, so it can't load logs ("STALL: no planks sawn for 5 minutes" at 77 min). The school sits after the forest on this route.

**Judgement.** No school default and no `SCHOOL_SHARE` value passes all nine seeds, and the failures move from seed to seed as the value changes: the harness is chaotic at this edge. It's not a one-value tune.

**Tried at the CoS's request: "holding never blocks loading"** (not on the branch; the patch, with its tests, is `docs/agents/qa/2026-11-01-hold-rule.patch`).
- **The rule:** nothing is held for a Research-first school in a wagon the train loads on the way there:
  - routed trains: the pickup stops before the school (`Later.loads`);
  - hand-run trains: the depots before it, the points as they lie (`reachOf` in travel order);
  - the engine card says "Dropping planks here: the wagons are needed for logs before the school".
- **Unit tests** (`workshop-unload.test.ts`, 3 new): fail before, pass after.
- **Balance with the full rule: 5/9 fail.**
  - 2027: gap 71.6 min.
  - Seed 1: research stall 36.8 min.
  - Seeds 4 and 9 collapse: 4 researched, no City, research stalled 126–135 min. Before routes, the hand-run train never reaches the farm built at the line's end, so Mills never gets its grain. The rule changed the early game (first research at 5.6 min, not 4.4) and the run diverged from there.
  - Seed 5: gap 61.0 min.
- **Balance with routed trains only: 3/9**, the same seeds as main (2027 stall 15.3; seeds 5 and 6 gaps 71.2 and 65.5). It makes no difference to the bands. On seed 5 the routed train only drops at the stop it's heading for, and its route comes to the city after the forest. So the planks are already in the wagons when it gets to the forest.
- **Taken out** after both runs, as the CoS asked for numbers before any more tuning. No band was widened.

**The CoS's decision: 3/9 accepted for this release as known failures, no band widened. Filed as issue #20** with the numbers and the design options; the CoS takes the choice to the user.

Options (in #20):
1. A way to drop or refuse held goods at a pickup: a wagon holding goods for the school, at a stop with goods to load into it, gives up the hold (the held goods left on the platform as landed goods, say).
2. A cap on held goods per train (one wagon's worth, say), so the other wagons stay free to load.
3. Drop Research first: Shares with towns only, perhaps with `SCHOOL_SHARE` 0.8 (3/9, all near misses: City 8 min early, one research short, a gap 2 min over).

**Goals play-through** (`GOALS_LONG=1`): fast 19/23 and long 23/23 with invariants held, both on main before the workshop change and on the change (fast 32 s / 47 s; long 96 s / 101 s).

## 3. Play-test (headless Chromium, Game mode from a new game)

Run twice: on `1dd092f8` (before the polish) and on `8b915e95` (the polish, the delight animations, bridge-gap and snap-closer all merged). Results are from the second run unless marked.

`playtest.mjs` (a QA scratch script, not committed) at 390×844 and 844×390 with touch, and at 1280×800 with the mouse.
- **Harness shortcuts:** the starter line laid by the game's simulated player; trains put down with `addTrain` and routed with `editLine`; the school and workshop placed with `editor().place`; the fast-forward.

- **Track and the snap:** a straight dragged from the tray and dropped beside a station's free end.

  - In landscape the play-test's drop missed. A probe showed the drag picks up fine (`drag-piece` all the way); the drop point there was a farm field, and the game said "That's a farm field — go around it". A harness artifact.
- **Stuck card and Show:**

  - on the first card of the two, three times (landscape twice, portrait once), the view target hadn't moved after Show. The play-test logs only the view target, not where each train stands, and two head-on trains may stand together already framed. Unconfirmed; e2e:touch's blocker checks, which measure Show on screen, pass.
- **Workshop and school chips** switch by taps, with the right lines. With a school on Research first, the workshop's Takes all says "…after your school's research (it's set to Research first)".
- **Delight animations** (after the polish): every one that started ran a fixed number of times, and none loops. - **Bridges over gaps:** not play-tested by me; the starter map has no one-square gap in a ridge to try it on. The landing's `bridge-gap.test.ts` and `support.test.ts` pass on the merged head (27/27), and its author swept 432 camera poses headless.

## 5. Performance (the polish: backdrop blur removed)

`perf.mjs` (QA scratch): 390×844 phone, headless SwiftShader. The starter line laid, two trains, main-thread time from CDP `Performance.getMetrics`, frame time from rAF deltas. Two runs of the polish build, one of main.

| | main | polish |
|---|---|---|
| idle 20 s: frames drawn | 0 | 0, 0 |
| idle: main thread busy | 3.3–4.1% | 3.2–3.6% |
| 2 trains running: frame p50 / p90 | 1000 / 1033–1050 ms | 900–917 / 1133–1167 ms |
| running, engine card and ☰ menu open: p50 | 983–1000 ms | 900–983 ms |
| idle after closing: frames in 20 s | 2–3 | 3 |

No regression. Under SwiftShader a frame costs about a second either way, all in the GPU process, so the blur's real cost (GPU compositing behind the menu and cards) only shows on a phone. Measure on a device to see the gain.
