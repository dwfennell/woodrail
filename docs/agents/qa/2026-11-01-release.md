Research pace is up with Research first. With the default Shares with towns it's about level, but stalls get longer.

**Cause of the big town gaps (seed 5, main now; `BALANCE_CARGO=76-84`).** Thimble Cross becomes a city at 73.6 min and sits at 0/22 planks until after 145 min. The other towns are full, the forest has 12 logs and the sawmill has planks. The train passes the city with 4 planks that the research (Signals, 4) lacks, kept for the school under Research first. Those planks stay in its two timber wagons round the line, through the forest, so it can't load logs ("STALL: no planks sawn for 5 minutes" at 77 min). The school sits after the forest on this route.

**Judgement.** No school default and no `SCHOOL_SHARE` value passes all nine seeds, and the failures move from seed to seed as the value changes: the harness is chaotic at this edge. It's not a one-value tune.

**Tried at the CoS's request: "holding never blocks loading"** (not on the branch; the patch, with its tests, is `docs/agents/qa/2026-11-01-hold-rule.patch`).
- **The rule:** nothing is held for a Research-first school in a wagon the train loads on the way there:
  - routed trains: the pickup stops before the school (`Later.loads`);
  - hand-run trains: the depots before it, the points as they lie (`reachOf` in travel order);
  - the engine card says "Dropping planks here: the wagons are needed for logs before the school".
- **Unit tests** (`workshop-unload.test.ts`, 3 new): fail before, pass after.
- **Balance with the full rule: 5/9 fail.**
  - 2027: gap 71.6 min.
  - Seed 1: research stall 36.8 min.
  - Seeds 4 and 9 collapse: 4 researched, no City, research stalled 126–135 min. Before routes, the hand-run train never reaches the farm built at the line's end, so Mills never gets its grain. The rule changed the early game (first research at 5.6 min, not 4.4) and the run diverged from there.
  - Seed 5: gap 61.0 min.
- **Balance with routed trains only: 3/9**, the same seeds as main (2027 stall 15.3; seeds 5 and 6 gaps 71.2 and 65.5). It makes no difference to the bands. On seed 5 the routed train only drops at the stop it's heading for, and its route comes to the city after the forest. So the planks are already in the wagons when it gets to the forest.
- **Taken out** after both runs, as the CoS asked for numbers before any more tuning. No band was widened.

Options for the CoS or Game Mode:
1. A rule at the pickup itself: a wagon holding goods for the school, at a stop with goods to load into it, gives up the hold. That means somewhere to put the held goods (dropped on the platform as landed goods, say). A design call.
2. Accept 3/9 for this release (the gap misses aren't near: 65–71 min against 45), with a follow-up task.
3. Shares with towns at `SCHOOL_SHARE` 0.8: 3/9, all near misses (City 8 min early, one research short, a gap 2 min over). But it changes what every player gets by default.

**Goals play-through** (`GOALS_LONG=1`): fast 19/23 and long 23/23 with invariants held, both on main before the workshop change and on the change (fast 32 s / 47 s; long 96 s / 101 s).
