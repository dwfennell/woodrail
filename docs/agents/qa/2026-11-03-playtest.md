# QA — play-test 2026-11-03

Branch `claude/qa-2026-11-03` from origin/main `1a6d4cb`. Run on a 4-core cloud container, headless Chromium (SwiftShader) driven by Playwright, phone context 390×844, touch, DPR 2 (and 844×390 for landscape). Dev server (`vite`), so no production build was played.

## Verdict

**PASS WITH NOTES.** No page errors and no console errors in any run (the only console line is three's `THREE.Clock` deprecation warning, on every load). A new game plays from the title to 14 of 24 goals, and the recently landed features (station clocks, research queue, routes, signals, feedback, fullscreen) all do what they're for. Nothing blocks. The findings are UI friction and polish: the ☰ menu left open over other views, emoji the icon font doesn't cover, the research board cramped on a phone, and a few wording and layout nits.

## Fixes on this branch

None. Nothing I found was a safe one-liner.

## Findings, by severity

Screenshots are in `docs/agents/qa/shots/2026-11-03-*.jpg`.

### 1. MEDIUM — the ☰ menu stays open over other views
- **Shot:** `menu-left-open-over-map`.
- **Repro:** new or continued game, 390×844. Tap ☰, then tap the Map button (top bar). The map opens and the menu stays open on top of it, hiding the right half of the map and its place labels. On desktop, Esc doesn't close the menu either: `main.ts:4788` closes only the map and the board on Escape, and the hud has no Escape handler for the menu.
- **Expected:** opening another view (Map, Research), or Esc, closes the menu.

### 2. MEDIUM — emoji the icon font doesn't draw (the "SVG icons instead of emoji" work)
- **Shot:** `emoji-feedback-chips` (🐞 Bug and 💡 Idea drawn by the phone's emoji font, unlike every other icon).
- **The gaps:** I compared every pictographic character in `src/**/*.ts` against `src/icons/u*.svg`. These are not covered:

  | emoji | where |
  |---|---|
  | 🐞 💡 | `ui/feedback-sheet.ts:97-98` |
  | 🚫 | `game/warehouse-text.ts:26,61,79` (the warehouse "takes none" lines) |
  | 🚐 🚌 | `game/blueprints.ts:296-297` (the vans and buses research) |

- **Text symbols, fine as text:** ⬆ ⬇ (route-plan text), ★ ☆ ✕ ✎ ☰.
- **Repro:** ☰ → Report a bug or give feedback (a build with `VITE_FEEDBACK_URL`).

### 3. MEDIUM — the research board is cramped on a phone when a ticket is open
- **Shot:** `board-ticket-covers-list`.
- **Repro:** 390×844, with something researching, tap the research pill. The ticket for the current research opens on the lower ~60% of the board, and `.board-body` is a 299–308 px window onto 2340 px of nodes.
  - Nodes lower down (Train routes, at y≈821) sit under the ticket.
  - A swipe that starts on the ticket doesn't scroll the list.
  - A tap aimed at a node just above the ticket's edge lands on the ticket. My harness started the wrong research (Engine shed, 100 coins) this way.
- **Ideas:** a collapsible ticket, or a shorter ticket until the player picks a node.

### 4. LOW-MEDIUM — a wagon dropped on the engine's front isn't coupled to its free end
- **Shot:** `flatbed-coupled` (the engine at Elderwick's end, after the drop on its rear worked). The refused drop is from the log only.
- **Repro:** run the first engine until it stops at the end of the line ("Turning back: the track ends here"), then Stop. Drag a Flatbed wagon from Trains › Wagons onto the engine's front half. You get "Not enough track there", and nothing is coupled.
- **Cause:** `dropTrain` → `joinTrain` (`interaction.ts:4801-4824`) tries only the train's nearer end.
- **Why it matters:** the goal says "Drag a flatbed wagon onto your engine to couple it". A new player's engine often stands at a buffer, and the drop works only on its rear half.
- **Suggestion:** try the far end when the near end has no room.

### 5. LOW-MEDIUM — Sandbox: half the tabs are hidden under the Draw/Clear chips
- **Shots:** `sandbox-tabs-under-chips`, `sandbox-tabs-swiped`.
- **Repro:** Sandbox › New map at 390×844. The tray has 10 tabs in a 246 px scroll strip: Track, Trains, Buildings, Nature, then Town at x 255–303, under the Draw chip at x 266–322. Only a "T" shows. Vehicles, People & animals, Machines, Roads and Land are off to the right with no hint that the strip scrolls. A swipe on the strip does scroll it.
- My tap on "Town" landed on Draw/Clear, which is what a player aiming at the sliver would get.

### 6. LOW — two "Learned …" toasts for each research when the queue moves on
- From the log; no shot.
- **Repro:** queue Switches, then Tank engine (Add to queue), and bring the planks. The toasts, one after the other:
  1. "Learned switches! Make a switch or a Y switch at the workshop · New goal: …"

- The same happens for Junctions and loops: "Learned junctions and loops!…", then "Learned Junctions and loops! Next: Signals".
- **Cause:** `main.ts:2047` lowercases the label and `main.ts:2126` doesn't. The second toast could be just "Next: Tank engine".

## What held

- **Errors:** no page errors and no console errors in about 40 scripted sessions, Game and Sandbox.
- **Idle:** paused, with a station clock in view, 0 frames in 30 s and the main thread 1.6% busy (CDP `TaskDuration`). Running: 5 frames in 15 s of SwiftShader time.
- **Early game:** with the economy running for real, logs were loaded, planks delivered, Larkfield became a village then a town, Elderwick likewise, and the coins rose (0 → 318).
  - The rewards sheet picks by taps.
  - The school and the workshop each replace a straight ("Straight back in the box").
- **Research queue:**
  - The flow: Research, then Add to queue ("Next up, after Switches"), then "2nd in line, after Tank engine", with ✕ Remove and Research now.
  - The queue runs in order: Switches → Tank engine → Quicker cranes.
  - When the queue empties, "Larkfield school is idle: pick what to learn next" is shown once.
  - The pill shows the goods (0/3), then the study clock (⏱ 20 s), which runs on game time.
- **Routes:**
  - The "Train routes" toast says "Tap a train, then Plan a route".
  - Plan a route, a tap on Larkfield, a tap on the Forest yard, Done: the stops become "Red line" 1 and 2, the map shows numbered discs, and the goal "Give a train a route" ticks.
  - **Not tried here:** the shortcut a track edit makes (`route-edit.test.ts` covers it), and the camera Follow button.
- **Signals:** `e2e:signals` all pass on main (placement by drag and by tool, the card, holding by hand, the payoff on a line of two trains, an old save's piece).
- **Station clocks:**
  - In-game, `clockView.minute` matched the container's time (22:01 UTC, minute 1321), with 5 station clocks and 2 market-hall clocks.
  - `e2e:station-clock` passes in Game and Sandbox: the hands show the time, 1 frame at each minute and 0 between.
- **Fullscreen:** ☰ → Fullscreen makes `document.fullscreenElement` the page, and the item turns into "Leave fullscreen". In landscape, the top bar has the fullscreen button too.
- **Feedback:** see 15. The sheet's copy and toggles match `docs/feedback.md`, and "Include my map" is locked on for a bug.
- **SVG icons:** goods, coin, book, timer, gift, bell, signals and tray icons render from the font. The exceptions are in 2.
- **Sandbox:** the bridge, kit and building shelves open; a straight drops on open ground; the kits refuse with a correct flip hint. `e2e:kits` all pass.
- **Delight:** `e2e:life` all pass (the first train's marker, the forest's axe, 0 frames paused).
- **Landscape 844×390:** the HUD has room for every chip (Draw, Clear, Turn, Flip, Map, Land, Undo, Redo, Play, Sound, Fullscreen, Menu), and the engine card fits (317×316 px, scrolls 42 px).
