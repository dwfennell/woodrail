## Findings, by severity

Screenshots are in `docs/agents/qa/shots/2026-11-04-*.jpg`.

### 1. MEDIUM-HIGH (performance) — Sandbox's opening view never goes idle
- **Shot:** `sandbox-idle-forest-working` (the view the Sandbox opens on: the map's sawmill and forest yards are near the middle).
- **Numbers:** production build, `node tools/perf/idle-frames.mjs`, with nothing else running.

  | measure | Sandbox (`#seed-7`) | Game |
  |---|---|---|
  | `idle-frames.mjs`, 20 s | 12–15 frames, 354–371% of a core (four runs) | 0 frames, 8.9% |
  | 90 s after load instead of 10 | 15 frames, 371% | — |
  | second visit (tray pictures cached, 0 `readPixels`) | still 14 frames in 20 s | — |

  The CPU figures are first visits, so the tray pictures' `readPixels` (below) is part of them; I measured only frames on the second visit. A frame takes about 1 s under SwiftShader, so 14 frames in 20 s is drawing every frame. On a phone it would be 60 fps with nothing happening.
- **Cause, found with probes:**
  - Over 15 s, the only frame-loop source that asked for frames was `depotView.update`, which returned "moving" 9–11 times. The others never did: road vehicles, boats, crossings, signal marks, touches, flourishes, director, camera and trains.
  - Both map depots have empty piles and there are no trains, so the cranes aren't working (`craneMove` needs a halted train).
  - That leaves the industries' motes (`depot-view.ts:1353-1364`). They run while `economy.working(site.depot)`.
  - For the forest (makes logs, uses nothing), `working` is true while its pile has room. In Sandbox its pile stays 0 (piles all 0 after 35 s), so it works, and asks for frames, forever while within crane reach of the camera.
  - In Game the forest's pile fills to its cap and it stops, which is why Game idles at 0.
- **Repro:** open Sandbox at `#seed-7`, Esc, wait, and count `woodrail.framesDrawn` over 20 s.
- **Expected:** no frames when nothing happens. For example, motes only in Game, only while goods actually come of the work, or just a few seconds after a change.
- **Also, first visit only:** after the start-up tray pictures are saved, opening shelves draws more pictures. That was 37 `readPixels` in 20 s, with the main thread 96% in `readPixels` while it lasts (the CPU profiler: 9.6 s of 10 s). It's 0 on a second visit.

### 2. MEDIUM — trees stand on a new harbour's yard and quay
- **Shot:** `trees-on-harbour-yard` (two firs and a ball tree through the plank stacks and the crane's yard). Also `harbour-card-boat-tied-up`.
- **Repro:**
  1. Game, seed 7: Harbours learned, a harbour made at the workshop.
  2. Drag the Harbour from Industries to the river bank north-west of the sawmill (pose x −1728, z −252, facing west). It's placed and "✓ Open a harbour" ticks.
  3. The map's own trees stay standing inside the yard: grid `FEAT_TREE` at depot (t 90–180, l −106…92).
- **Cause:** `treesAside` (`landscape/trees-aside.ts`) moves trees only off track sample points (`trackPoints`). A depot's yard, wider than the track, isn't in them.
- **Scope:** the harbour's yard is the widest. Any industry dropped onto trees likely shows the same; I didn't confirm it for the others.

### 4. LOW-MEDIUM — two engines on the starter line: both stop, nothing moves
- **Shot:** `two-trains-both-waiting`.
- **Repro:**
  1. Game, seed 7, the starter line (one track, no loop). The box has 2 Engines and 3 Flatbeds; the goal "Run two trains at once" appears with Switches.
  2. Drag both engines on, a flatbed onto each.
  3. Plan the first's route by taps (Forest yard → Sawmill → Larkfield). Give the second **Red line**, and Go both.
- **What happens:**
  - The toast says "This line has room for 1 train. Lay a passing loop, or take one off the track."
  - Willow, held between Elderwick and the forest: "Stuck: this line has a train too many. Add a passing loop".
  - Teasel, at the sawmill end: "Waiting: lay 1 more passing loop for 2 trains".
  - Over 240 game seconds, 0 goods are loaded or delivered: the held train stands on the runner's only way to the forest.
  - After **Remove** on Willow, the line delivers (180 s: 9 logs loaded, 3 planks delivered, the four goals tick).
- **Notes:** it's designed (the accepted "train too many" limit). But it's the natural move with the box's two engines, and the line stops dead. Neither card offers the quick fix (Leave or Remove); only the passing loop is named. The fuzz's 2 stalled seeds are this case ("too-many").

### 5. LOW-MEDIUM — Sandbox: "Needs trees nearby" can't be met by planting trees
- **Shot:** `harvester-needs-trees`.
- **Repro:**
  1. Sandbox, seed 7. Drag a Harvester to the open grass by the start; its card says "Needs trees nearby: 4 of 5".
  2. Drag two Lollipop trees from Nature › Trees beside it (both placed: props 47 and 48).
  3. The card still says 4 of 5.
- **Cause:** `SITE_RULES.forest.counts` reads `grid.feature === FEAT_TREE`, and Nature-tab trees are props. Only the Land tab's moving of map trees helps.
- **Suggestion:** count tree props, or say "move trees here with Land".
