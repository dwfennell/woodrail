**Woodrail 1.1.8: swap engines, choose where grand projects go, and saves that are never lost**

This update lets you change a train's engine without rebuilding it, lets you choose where a grand project goes and
move it later, and makes sure a save is never lost. The train dispatcher (beta) gets smarter, and lots of smaller
things you reported are fixed.

**Swap engine**
- A stopped train's card has a new Engine button. Pick another engine from the list, and the train keeps its name,
  line, cars and loads. Engines you haven't learned yet show what to research first.

**Grand projects: you choose the spot**
- Start puts the project's plot up where the game suggests. Drag it where you like, then tap Build here to pay.
  ⟳ suggests another spot, and Cancel leaves it.
- Hold a finger on a project's plot to move it later, anywhere near its city.
- A plot never lands on a yard, a road or a farm's fields, and trees keep off it.

**Your games are safe**
- A save the game can't open is never written over. You're told on the title screen, and it's kept in Your games.
- A save with a warehouse stacked high (Lift and load) opens again.
- Your games has Load a map from text: paste a "Copy map for a bug report" text, and it's added as a new game beside
  the others.
- After we put out an update, a game left open no longer says "Couldn't fetch that part of the game", and Research
  always opens again after a moment offline.

**Goods and wagons**
- The mine brings up two coal and one ore at a time, so coal is quicker. A mine full of ore keeps bringing up coal.
- With the Smithy, a wagon's card says it's a heavy wagon that holds 5. Its 4th and 5th logs, planks and iron now
  sit neatly on a flatbed.
- A train carrying goods along its line is no longer told to add a wagon for some other good at a stop.
- A town's card says first that it buys any good: full pay for what it needs, half for others.

**Yards with more than one track**
- Second track is now one straight beside the yard, under the crane. The card says how to lengthen it for a train
  longer than the yard.
- A second track joined to another line now counts for the trains on that line.
- A train no longer waits for ever at a red signal set by its own tail on a ring.

**Train dispatcher (beta)**
- Still a switch in ☰, off unless you turn it on. When it's on, it plans which train goes first on shared track.
- On a single line it now lets one train through at a time, and the waiting train's card says so: "Waiting for
  Willow to clear the single line".
- It sets the points for the trains it lets go, and no longer sends a train onto points set against it or nose to
  nose with another.
- Many smaller fixes to how trains wait at points, in dead ends and in yards with more than one track. Please keep
  telling us how it goes with the "Report a bug" button.

**Fixes**
- Fixed: an underpass filled in after a reload.
- Fixed: cards ran under the tray on big screens. A place's card keeps its ✕ and title at the top as you scroll.
- Fixed: the ⓘ on the research board showed as a strange character on a Mac.
- Fixed: a train standing on points could wait for a train that wasn't there.

Thanks for playing, and keep the bug reports coming from the in-game "Report a bug" button. They go straight to us.
