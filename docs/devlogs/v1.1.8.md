**Woodrail 1.1.8: towns in rows, cloud saves on itch.io, and more of the map on phones**

Towns now grow as streets of joined houses and shops, your railway can now be saved online when you play here on
itch.io, and phones get more room for the map. The train dispatcher (beta) also got a lot smarter.

**Towns in rows**
- A town's houses and shops now stand in joined rows along its streets, and fill the rows before the town spreads.
- Landmarks and town places have their own buildings made for their plots, and the station house matches them.
- Towns in your saved games are rebuilt in rows when you load them.

**Cloud saves on itch.io**
- Your railway now saves online here too. The first time you build, you're given a code: keep it, and you can load
  your railway with it on any device.
- On iPhone and iPad, itch.io forgets your railway when you leave the page. The title screen now says so, and
  **Load with a code** brings it back in a couple of taps.
- The Cloud save card shows your code first, with Copy and Share.
- An earlier copy of your railway is kept online, from before you last sat down to play, and the card can take you
  back to it.
- Clearer wording when two copies of your railway disagree: it says which one you're playing and where the other went.

**More of the map on phones**
- On a phone held upright, the tray folds down to its tabs with the pull above it, giving the map much more room.
- The goal bar is a single line.

**Keeping your railway safe**
- Your games are backed up as you play. In ☰ Your games, Restore an earlier version lists the copies by date, with
  how many trains and pieces each has.
- A save that would replace a real railway with an almost empty one is held back, and the railway is kept.

**Trains and the dispatcher (beta)**
- The train dispatcher handles many more busy layouts: trains step aside into spare track, take turns at the ends
  of single lines, and stay clear of hand-run trains.
- A signal shows red while the train standing at it is being held, so you can see why it's waiting.
- A train's card says when it's held with no way on, or when no track leads to its next stop.
- A line's stop on a separate stretch of track is skipped, and the card says so.

**Boats**
- Barges carry any goods, not only bulk ones, and boat stops have Drop and Pick up.

**Smaller things**
- Cards share one layout and width, and never cover what they're about.
- Places have one name everywhere; docks are named after the nearest town.
- Grand Central's last stage says what counts.
- Fixed: a yard's extra tracks counted only when they're beside it.
- Fixed: some trains that stayed stuck with the dispatcher on.
