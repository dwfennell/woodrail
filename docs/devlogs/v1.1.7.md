**Woodrail 1.1.7: help when you're stuck, one-way signals, and a train dispatcher to try**

This update adds tips that spot common jams and say how to fix them, adds one-way signals, makes stuck trains'
cards clearer, and adds a new train dispatcher you can try out. It also gives the railway's own buildings and
trains a new look.

**Help when you're stuck**
- The game now notices some common problems as you play and shows a 💡 tip under the goals. Show where takes you to
  the spot, and Learn more explains the fix. The tip goes away once you've fixed it.
- Trains queuing for a whole stretch of track: the tip shows where a signal would let them follow each other.
- Signals too close together, with no room for a train to wait between them.
- Trains stuck nose to nose on a single line: a passing loop would let them pass. A new Learn more sheet explains
  passing loops.
- The game is paused: if you open a train's card a while after pausing, the tip points to the play button.
- A stop a train can't reach: the tip names the line and the stop it skips.
- A train too long for its yard: it says to take a car off or add a second track.
- Turn tips off and on from ☰ Tips.

**Train dispatcher (beta)**
- A new switch in ☰: Train dispatcher (beta). It's off unless you turn it on, and it's set per game.
- When it's on, the game plans which train goes first, so trains don't block each other on shared track.
- It's a beta, so it won't always get things right yet. Try it on busy maps with lots of shared single track, and
  please tell us how it goes with the "Report a bug" button. You can turn it off again at any time.

**Signals**
- One way: a new tile on the Signals shelf. It puts down a single signal facing the way you drag it (or draw with
  the Signal tool). A pair's card can make it One way only, and a single signal's card can make it Both ways.
- Signals now stand only on open ground beside the track, never inside a hill's side or a house.

**Clearer cards for stuck trains**
- A train waiting behind two trains that face each other now names those two trains, not the one just ahead.
- When two lines' trains meet nose to nose, the card says the track they share has no passing place long enough,
  and what would fix it for good.
- A train waiting while others go first says so, with how many have gone: "Waiting for the line to Teasel End: 2
  trains have gone first".
- Trains are named by their line's name, as you wrote it.

**A new look**
- The railway's yard buildings, stations, engine shed and roundhouse have been restyled to match the towns.
- Engines and wagons have more detail: the express is now a streamliner, and the coaches, vans and wagons have
  doors, windows and buffers.
- The cathedral has new towers, spires and a west front with a rose window.

**Fixes**
- Fixed: a train could keep heading for the wrong stop after the plan to let trains swap places was dropped.
- Fixed: long trains no longer try to turn back in a dead end shorter than they are. They skip a stop they can't
  reach that way, and their card says why.
- Fixed: a yard's Second track is now laid shorter past something in the way, instead of being refused. The card
  says what's in the way.
- Fixed: new streets in a growing town now show up straight away, not only after a reload.

Thanks for playing, and keep the bug reports coming from the in-game "Report a bug" button. They go straight to us.
