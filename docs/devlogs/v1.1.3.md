**Woodrail 1.1.3: fewer stuck trains**

A small update, mostly fixes from your bug reports. Thank you for sending them.

**Signals and stuck trains**
- Trains heading into a passing loop now take the empty road first, so two trains no longer block each other there.
- A train waiting at a signal now always shows red. Before, it could stop a hair past the signal, which then turned
  green while the train stayed put.
- A signal placed inside a passing loop, where a waiting train blocks the points, now says so on its card and on the
  stuck trains' cards. It tells you where to move it.
- Points changed for a train you're driving by hand now stay set until it has gone over them.
- Signals are tidier: their lamps are only on the front.

**Easier to read**
- Each town's sign now shows the good it's waiting for, with the right picture and a count that fits on the board.
- On the engine card, "Throw away" is now called "Delete cargo".

**Looks**
- Fixed flickering surfaces on models, including the mine's rock hill.

Thanks for playing! You can send bug reports from the in-game "Report a bug" button. They go straight to us.
