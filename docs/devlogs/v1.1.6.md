**Woodrail 1.1.6: trains that load in place, fewer jams, and a new way to plan lines**

This update changes how trains load, teaches them to stop jamming each other on shared track, redesigns how you plan
lines, and lets you keep several games at once.

**Trains load standing still**
- A train now pulls up once at a yard and the crane runs along it, loading or unloading each wagon in turn. No more
  shunting forward a wagon at a time.
- At the end of a line, a train pulls right up to the buffers and turns back once it's worked.
- If a train is too long for its yard and stands on the points while it loads, its card says so.

**Fewer jams**
- Before a train is let onto a stretch of track, the game looks ahead to check that every train nearby can still
  make its next move. Trains no longer drive into spots they can't get out of.
- Trains meeting head-on on a single track that several lines share now take turns instead of locking up.
- A train waiting at a red signal says why on its card.
- If a train keeps waiting on a long single track that lines share, its card says where, and that a passing loop
  would help.

**Planning lines**
- A line's stops are drawn as one diagram with its trains on it, so you can see where each train is.
- Add a stop in the middle of a line: tap the + between two stops on the sheet, or the + peg on the map between them,
  then tap a place.
- Tap a new place into a line that's already running, and it goes where it fits best, not at the end. The sheet says
  where it went, with Move to end and Undo.
- A train's card shows its line read-only, with its stops as pegs. Tap a peg to open the line at that stop.
- Leave line: take a train off its line from its card. It stops where it is (clear of any points), and the line keeps
  running with its other trains. Put it back with Run a line.

**Passengers go somewhere**
- Passengers now want to go to a particular place, and board only a train whose line goes there. They stay aboard
  past other towns until they reach it.
- Fares are paid for the whole trip, so a long line pays for its length.
- Towns with attractions, like a museum or a park, draw more of the passengers. The town card lists what draws people.
- A town's card shows who is waiting and where they want to go. A train's card shows where its riders are going.
- If a line stops going where its riders want, they get off at the next town.

**Your games**
- Keep several games side by side. ☰ Your games lists them with pictures. Open, rename, copy or delete any of them.
- Continue opens the game you played last.

**Building**
- A yard's Second and Third track now lays just the track beside the yard. Join it to your line with Splits from the
  tray, instead of a ladder of crossovers.
- Points dropped from the tray onto a line splice into it.
- The starter map's lumber yard and sawmill can be picked up and moved like any other industry.

**Fixes**
- Fixed: viaducts and bridges over hills now fit the ground. Their arches follow the slope, and a viaduct crossing a
  hill's edge at an angle has one row of arches, not two.
- Fixed: the square a moved house left behind no longer blocks track, and undo can't stack two houses on one square.
- Fixed: a card's ✕ and Follow buttons no longer overlap the load row.
- Fixed: Swap ends, Decouple and Remove stay in view at the foot of a long train card on a phone.
- Fixed: cargo you delete at a yard isn't loaded straight back on.

Thanks for playing, and keep the bug reports coming from the in-game "Report a bug" button. They go straight to us.
