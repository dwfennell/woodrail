**Woodrail 1.1.5: the toy box, town cards and smarter trains**

This update gives the whole game a new wooden look, redesigns the town card, lets you turn pieces in finer steps,
and teaches trains to share track much better.

**The toy box look**
- Every card, menu, sheet and the tray now looks like the wooden box the railway came in: beech boards with the grain
  showing, maple tiles, stamped prices and dates, and one bright painted button for the main action on each panel.
- Buttons and tabs are at least a finger's height on a phone.
- A route on a train's or a boat's card is drawn as a little rail with its stops.

**Town cards**
- A town's card now has tabs: Grow, Build, Projects and Station. A yellow peg on a tab means something is ready there.
- "Next up" says the one thing the town needs most, with a button for it: a new route, a mill to find, a town hall to
  build or a grand project to start.
- Grand projects show their stages as pegs, with the goods each stage needs.
- In the Journal, an achievement you haven't started lists the cities you could build it in. Tap one to open that
  town's card at the project.

**Building**
- Turn a held piece in 15° steps: twist it with a second finger on a phone, or press Shift+R. Turn and R still step
  an eighth, and a piece twisted close to an eighth snaps onto it.
- New piece: the Split, a crossover with the second line missing at one end. It's on the Switch shelf, learned with
  Junctions and loops.
- The research board shows only what's in the game. The rows marked "Soon" are gone.

**Trains share track much better**
- A train now books the track only as far as the next signal, not all the way to its next stop. Trains can follow
  each other much more closely along a signalled line.
- Trains on the same line can follow each other into a dead end and turn round behind each other, instead of one
  waiting for the other to leave. A signal lets the second train in behind the first.
- A train following another can also enter a signal block behind a train that is stopped at the far signal, if
  there's room.
- A train steps aside into a spare siding or platform to let another one past.
- When trains on a line with no signals would otherwise jam, they work out a plan and take turns to get through.
- On a ring with no passing loop, all the trains go round the same way.
- A passing loop keeps its last free track for a train coming the other way, so three trains can share one loop.
- When two trains face each other between signals, the card says so and suggests what to do, instead of telling you
  to add a signal.

**Also new**
- The "Deliver planks" goal now says to lay a Curve or use Draw, and Show me opens the Curve shelf.
- On Flat and Gentle maps, the ground for your first lines is level.
- Fixed: a tap on the ☰ menu while it was opening could land on the item above.

Thanks for playing! You can send bug reports from the in-game "Report a bug" button. They go straight to us.
