**Woodrail 1.1.4: lines, new maps and a wiki**

This update makes routes easier to plan, lets you choose the land a new game starts on, and adds a wiki.

**Lines**
- Routes are now lines. A line has its own sheet: its stops drawn as a line, and a Trains tab for the trains that run it.
- To make a line, tap places' buildings on the map. A town needs a station first, and the sheet says so.
- Tap a stop to see what's dropped and picked up there, and to change it. Drag a stop to move it.
- The new Lines list (☰ Lines) shows every line, with the ones that have a problem first, what each carries and how
  many loads it moved this month.
- A line stays when it has no trains. Each line also gets a name no other line has.
- An engine on no line offers "Run a line…" on its card, with the nearest lines first.

**A new game, your way**
- New game is now a screen of its own. Pick a map seed, how hilly the land is (Flat, Gentle, Hilly or Mountainous),
  how many rivers and lakes, and whether there's a sea.
- New maps are gentler by default, with open ground to start on.
- ☰ shows your map's code. Type it into the seed box to make the same world again.

**Trains and signals**
- Path signals now show the way of the nearest train, not of whichever train asked first.
- Trains no longer turn round in the middle of a line to get out of each other's way.
- A train keeps the track it's standing on, so points can't be switched under another train's wagons.

**Also new**
- A Woodrail wiki, with pages on every industry, engine, wagon and research step. Open it from ☰, an industry's card
  or a research ticket.
- Cloud saves now turn on by themselves once you start building, and the title screen offers "Continue from cloud".
- The ☰ menu scrolls on small phones, so you can always reach the bottom.
- The Grand Exhibition and the Cathedral have new, more detailed models. The observatory, the boats, the airship and
  the grand projects have smoother curves.
- The game uses less memory and runs a little faster.

Thanks for playing! You can send bug reports from the in-game "Report a bug" button. They go straight to us.
