**Woodrail 1.1.2: signals, boats, and a lot more to build**

**Signals you place yourself**
- Signals now have their own shelf. Place them in pairs, one facing each way; a pair moves and comes off as one set.
- Tap a signal to open its card. The card says what it's doing and why a train is waiting ("Waiting for Bramble to
  clear the line to the mill").
- Trains on shared track take turns. Routed trains give way, back into passing loops, and get out of dead ends
  instead of jamming.
- A new "Learn more" sheet explains how signals work.

**Water transport**
- River tugs, barges, cargo ships and a train ferry, built at yards from goods your trains bring in.
- New landings, ferry slips and a lighthouse.

**New things to build**
- Post: towns send mail, mail vans carry it to a post office, and it goes out to other towns.
- New goods chains: jam, chocolate, and flowers → paint → toys.
- New track: slips, the double three-way, and turntables you can work.
- Depot upgrades: industries grow to 2 and 3 tracks.
- New places: a mountain rack railway with a lookout halt, a stone viaduct, a lift bridge, an observatory, an
  airport, an airship mast, a metro under towns, and a wind farm.
- New carriages: night sleepers and an observation car.
- Paint your own engines and wagons.

**Grand projects and secrets**
- Grand projects: the Great Exhibition, a Cathedral, the Royal Train, a World's Fair wheel that slowly turns, and
  Grand Central. They're tracked as achievements in the Journal.
- Six secret sites are hidden on the map. Lay track to one to find it, and it opens as a tourist halt.

**Easier to read**
- The engine card is reorganised. It also says why a train won't load and which wagon each good needs.
- Coaches and goods vans now look different from above.
- New features come with light tips the first time you meet them.

**Faster on phones**
- Less to download at first, less GPU memory, and simpler distant scenery.
- The game draws almost nothing when nothing is moving.
- It recovers if your phone drops the graphics context.

Thanks for playing, and keep the bug reports coming from the in-game "Report a bug" button. They go straight to us.
