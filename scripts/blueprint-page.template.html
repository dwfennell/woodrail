How it works

  • Learning: towns offer blueprints as they grow (village: track, town: industries, city: advanced). Pick one free or take a crate. The rest wait on the board at their own price. Schools have ideas, colleges twice as often.
  • A web, not a line: many blueprints need two or three others, so different railways reach the same place by different roads. A stadium needs a sports field and busy stations; the airport needs electricity and trade; the rocket needs five lines.
  • Discovery: a new player sees the key blueprints. The rest show as "?" until something next to them is learned, and the University shows everything left.
  • Coming soon: in the game, blueprints we haven't built would show on the board as "coming soon" tags, so players can see where the railway is going.
  • Each one shows: a blueprint brings a building, car or piece you haven't seen, and most change how you play. Many need a model from the asset session; the dashed ones (school, library) go up in towns by themselves.
